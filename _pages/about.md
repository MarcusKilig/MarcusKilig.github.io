Hi, I’m Pujun Feng👋 
You may also call me Marcus 🐈‍⬛ 🐈‍⬛ 🐈‍⬛

I am currently a Research Assistant at [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn/) <img src='./images/pku.png' style="width: 2em; height:  2em;"> and an Algorithm and Model Development Intern at [Medin.ai](https://medin.ai/) <img src='./images/medin.png' style="width: 3em;">.


Previously, I obtained my Master’s degree in Big Data and Internet of Things from the Faculty of Applied Sciences at Macao Polytechnic University <img src='./images/mpu.png' style="width: 2.5em; height: 2.5em;">, where I was also involved in model development and multiple collaborative research projects at the [GEM Lab](https://gemlab.site/index.html)  (Generalized Electric Medicine Group) <img src='./images/gem.png' style="width: 2em">.


My research focuses on:

📎 AI for Healthcare/Medicine 🏥

📎 AI for Science 🔬

📎 Medical Foundation Models 🧠

📎 Multimodal Learning 🎯

📎 Collaborative Intelligent Agents 🤖
